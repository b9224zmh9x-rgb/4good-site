Privacy

Your food is personal.

Effective October 19, 2026.

This policy describes 4GOOD’s current food-management and cooking-coordination app. For privacy questions or requests, contact efrueh.4good@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process account information such as your email address, account identifier and profile name; groceries and quantities you save; purchase dates and estimated freshness dates; private meal records and confirmed ingredient use; and the optional profile preferences you provide.

If you use community features, we also process kitchen memberships, friend requests and relationships, approved cooking availability, selected ingredient names, invitations and responses, posts, blocks and safety reports. Service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and operational logs to deliver and protect the service.

Receipt and food-photo analysis

When you request a receipt scan, the receipt image is sent through a 4GOOD server function to Google’s Gemini API for grocery extraction and freshness estimates. When you choose food-photo analysis, the selected photos and your inventory’s ingredient names and amounts are sent to Google for suggestions. A requested shared-meal idea uses the ingredient names that participating friends chose to share.

4GOOD does not intentionally save receipt images or food-analysis photos in its app database or photo storage. This does not mean that Google or infrastructure providers retain no processing or security records. Google’s processing is subject to its Gemini API terms. We do not promise zero provider retention or make a blanket claim that provider training is disabled without verifying the applicable service configuration.

Avoid including payment details, addresses, faces or other sensitive information in photos. Review and correct AI output before saving it. Analysis does not automatically deduct food; deductions require your confirmation. Posting a photo is a separate action.

How sharing works

Your full inventory is private by default. Joining a kitchen lets permitted members see your profile name, but does not automatically make everyone friends. Accepted friends in a shared kitchen can see the cooking hours and ingredient names you explicitly share. Full calendar event details and pantry quantities are not disclosed through that friend-sharing feature.

Kitchen-wide ingredient offers intentionally share selected ingredient names with other kitchen members, including non-friends. Photos and captions you post are visible to the selected kitchen, not necessarily only your friends. Expiring image links can remain usable briefly after access changes; other people may retain screenshots or copies.

Optional calendar access is used on your device to suggest free periods. Approved availability and recurring hours, including timezone, are stored for matching. Recurring hours do not mean calendar conflicts are continuously checked. Kitchen codes do not verify university affiliation.

Providers, notifications and diagnostics

We use Supabase for accounts, database records, server functions and community-photo storage, and Google Gemini for requested AI features. Providers may process information in countries other than your own. See Supabase’s privacy information and Google’s terms above.

Freshness reminders are scheduled locally on your device with your permission and use generic notification text. Current optional journey diagnostics are temporary, bounded records held in app memory; they are not automatic session recordings. You can review, clear and deliberately share a diagnostic report. Developer testing programs such as TestFlight may separately collect diagnostics and feedback under Apple’s terms.

We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers in the current app. This static website has no added analytics scripts, advertising, forms or app login. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process visitor technical information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Saved account and food records are generally kept to provide your account and history until you remove them or delete your account. Expiring visibility is not the same as deletion: shared ingredient contributions are hidden after 14 days and kitchen offers after 48 hours, but underlying records and command history can remain. Operational and security records, safety reports, and provider backups may follow different retention requirements. We do not promise immediate erasure from backups or provider logs.

You can edit food records and initiate permanent account deletion in the app’s Settings. Account deletion is designed to remove associated app records and community photos under our control. Other people’s copies and provider records are not necessarily erased at the same time. For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, email support. We may need to verify that you control the account; do not send your password. Rights available to you depend on applicable law.

Children and safety

4GOOD is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact support. Food estimates and AI suggestions are not food-safety, nutritional, medical or allergy guarantees. Check food condition and dietary needs yourself, and share meeting or pickup details cautiously.

Changes and contact

We will update the policy date when practices change and provide additional notice where required. Contact 4Good at efrueh.4good@gmail.com.
