Support

Let’s get you unstuck.

For app help, privacy requests or community safety reports, email support@4-good.app. Support is provided by 4Good. This is not a continuously monitored emergency service.

Report a problem

Include your app version/build, device model, what you tried, and the message you saw. A screenshot is useful if you remove sensitive information first. Never send passwords, verification codes, API keys, complete receipts with payment details, or private meeting addresses.

Receipt or photo scan failed?

Use a clear, well-lit photo and check your connection. You can add groceries manually when receipt analysis is unavailable. Review quantities and units before saving. Food-photo analysis only suggests usage; confirm the amounts yourself.

A save is waiting for confirmation?

A timeout does not always mean the save failed. Use the saved action’s retry control instead of creating another copy. If confirmation still fails, contact support with the visible error and build number.

Freshness reminders

Allow notifications on your phone. In 4GOOD Settings, choose Enable freshness reminders to schedule eligible reminders for food already saved. Current reminders are scheduled for 10am local time on the day before the estimated use-by date; already-passed reminder times are skipped. Device notification settings and Focus modes can affect alerts. Freshness dates are estimates, not guarantees that food is safe.

Cooking with friends

Join the same kitchen, connect as friends, and each choose cooking hours and ingredients to share. Kitchen membership alone is not friendship. Check invitations inside Community; closed-app friend and dinner notifications are not included in the current house-test build.

Community safety

Use the app’s report and block controls where available, and email support to report inappropriate content or conduct. Include enough detail to identify the issue without sending unnecessary personal information. For an immediate threat or emergency, contact your local emergency services; do not wait for an app-support response.

Account and privacy requests

Account deletion is available in the app’s Settings. For help accessing, correcting or deleting your information, email support from the address associated with your account when possible. See our privacy policy for data-handling details.
